About Me

I am a Quality Assurance Engineer focused on automation-driven quality engineering. My experience spans web, API, mobile, and performance testing, with emphasis on building repeatable and maintainable test systems rather than isolated scripts.

My goal is to design automation that supports product stability, minimizes regression risk, and integrates seamlessly into development workflows.

I continuously refine my approach toward system-oriented QA — where quality assurance is embedded into the engineering lifecycle, not treated as a final checkpoint.

Current Focus

  • Building maintainable test suites with Page Object Model and GitHub Actions CI
  • Improving test maintainability and reusability
  • Strengthening regression coverage for critical user flows
  • Integrating automation into continuous testing workflows
  • Advancing toward system-level quality engineering
